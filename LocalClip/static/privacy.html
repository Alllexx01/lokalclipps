Privacy Policy

Effective: November 8, 2026

1. Data processed

Depending on the features you use, LokalClipps processes uploaded video and audio, public source URLs, transcripts, generated clips, captions, processing settings, and job metadata. When you connect TikTok, it also stores the authorization and refresh tokens returned by TikTok.

2. Purpose

Data is used only to import media, transcribe speech, select and render clips, show processing history, and publish a clip when requested or enabled by your automation settings.

3. Local processing and sharing

Transcription and clip generation run locally or on the self-hosted LokalClipps server. Data is not sold and is not used for advertising. A source host receives normal requests when you import a public URL. TikTok receives the clip, caption, publishing settings, and API credentials only when you connect TikTok or publish through its API.

4. Security

TikTok client secrets and access tokens are encrypted at rest. Login sessions use signed, HttpOnly cookies. Browser cookies are not extracted for automated publishing.

5. Retention

Video jobs are deleted according to the retention period selected in LokalClipps, which defaults to three days. TikTok tokens remain until you disconnect TikTok or remove the local configuration. Platform-side copies follow TikTok's own retention rules.

6. Your choices

You can delete local jobs, shorten retention, avoid connecting TikTok, or disconnect TikTok at any time. You can also request deletion or correction of data controlled by the service operator.

7. Contact

Privacy questions and requests can be submitted through the LokalClipps GitHub issue tracker.
